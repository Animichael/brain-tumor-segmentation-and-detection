About Us

Deep Learning Built for Radiology Workflows

Our platform pairs a U-Net segmentation network, trained to trace tumor boundaries at the pixel level, with a ResNet50 classifier that distinguishes glioma, meningioma, and pituitary tumors from healthy tissue. Together they give clinicians both a precise boundary and a confident diagnosis in the time it takes to review a single slice.

  • {{ icon('checkmark-circle-20-filled') }} Pixel-level tumor segmentation with U-Net
  • {{ icon('checkmark-circle-20-filled') }} Four-class ResNet50 classification with confidence scoring
  • {{ icon('checkmark-circle-20-filled') }} Results delivered directly into the clinician dashboard
